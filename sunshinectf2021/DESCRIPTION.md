## SunshineCTF 2021

Online, jeopardy-style CTF hosted by Hack@UCF (the Collegiate Cyber Defense Club at the University of
Central Florida) and run by the Knightsec team — the sixth annual SunshineCTF, held in affiliation with
BSides Orlando. Challenges span pwn, reversing, web, crypto, scripting, and misc. Flag format `sun{...}`.
